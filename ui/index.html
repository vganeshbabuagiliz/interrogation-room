Death at Larkspur Manor

Mr. Ashworth, owner of Larkspur Manor, was found dead in his study last night. The door had been locked from the inside. Four people were in the house, and every one of them has a prepared story. You are the detective. They have been brought in one at a time.

Your goal: work out who killed Ashworth, and break them. Get a confession (a suspect who cracks) while learning what the innocent ones are hiding.
  1. Question each suspect: where they were, what they saw, how they knew the victim.
  2. Investigate the scene and logs (type things like "check the boiler log" or "search the library") to collect evidence in your notebook.
  3. Confront them with evidence and their own earlier words. Contradictions are flagged for you.
  4. Watch the nervousness and stance in the trace: a suspect who deflects and lies under pressure is hiding something. Everyone has a secret, but only one is the killer's.
The suspects

Night-shift caretaker

Gruff, short sentences, avoids eye contact. Works nights at the manor and calls Ashworth "the owner".

Eleanor Voss

Housekeeper, twelve years

Prim and formal; hides worry behind manners and corrects you when she can.

Clara Ashworth

The victim's wife

Composed and icy. Answers questions with questions.

Her story: reading in the library from 9pm. Last saw her husband at dinner at 8pm.

Tobias Reed

Nephew and heir

Nervy, talks too fast, jokes when scared.

His story: alone in the billiard room from 9pm to 11pm. Had tea with his uncle at 4pm.
